PURINEX WEBSITE STARTER

Open index.html in a modern browser to preview the website.
The site is responsive and requires no installation or framework.

Before publishing:
- Replace sample prices with your real prices.
- Add your business email and official social links.
- The inquiry form prepares a message locally; it does not email or store submissions.

To publish, upload index.html to a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.
